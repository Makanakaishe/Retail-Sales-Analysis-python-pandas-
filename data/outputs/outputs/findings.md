## Data quality issues found and fixed

- rows: 6,120
- duplicate rows: 120

- non positive quantity: 60
- inconsistent category labels: 594
- slash format dates: 486

## Cleaning decisions

- Exact duplicate rows were dropped.
- Two date formats (ISO and day/month/year) were parsed separately to avoid mixing up day and month.
- Category labels were trimmed and normalised to title case.
- Missing cities were labelled `Unknown` and excluded from the city chart only.
- Rows with non-positive quantities were excluded, since they look like returns or entry errors. In a real project, ask the data owner which it is.
